### 2.2 FW-MAE 的物理意义

审稿人要求说明 FW-MAE 绝对值与实际物理预报误差的对应关系。以下是建议在 §6 或 §3 中增加的说明段落：

```latex
The forecast-weighted MAE (FW-MAE) is defined as
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{FW\text{-}MAE} = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{H}|} \sum_{h \in \mathcal{H}}
  w_h \cdot \mathrm{MAE}(\hat{y}_{t+h}, y_{t+h}),
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal{H}$ is the set of forecast horizons and $w_h$ are
horizon-dependent weights. In the present simulation, the primary
forecast target is 2-m air temperature, for which the TCN oracle
achieves a full-observation FW-MAE of $0.369$ (budget $B=1.70$).
This corresponds to a mean absolute temperature error of approximately
$0.37\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ at the one-step-ahead horizon, consistent
with the sub-degree accuracy reported for deep-learning Antarctic
temperature reconstruction \citep{Tran2026}.
The AoI scheduler's FW-MAE of $0.424$ corresponds to an additional
$\sim 0.055\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$ degradation relative to PD-PPO---a
practically meaningful difference in the context of Antarctic blowing-snow
onset prediction, where temperature thresholds near $-10\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
govern phase transitions in precipitation type \citep{Amory2020}.
```

**注意**：上述物理解释需要作者确认 FW-MAE 的归一化方式（是否已对原始量纲归一化）。若 FW-MAE 是对归一化后的残差计算，则需将 0.37°C 替换为实际反归一化后的温度误差。

## 三、审稿意见三：图 3 风速 ACF 验证失败的解释与修正

### 3.1 问题定位

审稿人指出图 3 中风速 ACF 验证失败，即合成数据的自相关函数与真实 AntAWS 数据不一致，违反了命题 3 的全部标准。这与 turn_46 分析中指出的"事件时序结构缺失"直接相关：当前仿真采用独立采样，无法复现风速的自相关结构（极地边界层风速通常具有显著的小时级自相关，ACF 在 lag=1h 时约为 0.7–0.9）。

### 3.2 解释路径（若不修正仿真器）

若本轮投稿不重新运行实验，可在 §4 中增加以下说明，将 ACF 失配定位为已知局限：

```latex
The synthetic time series generator reproduces the marginal distributions
of individual sensor channels but does not enforce inter-temporal
autocorrelation structure. Wind speed in Antarctic boundary-layer
conditions exhibits persistent autocorrelation (ACF $\approx 0.8$ at
lag $= 1\,\mathrm{h}$; \citealt{Amory2020}), which the current
independent-sampling scheme cannot replicate. As a consequence,
Proposition~3 (stationarity of the synthetic process) holds for the
marginal distribution but not for the second-order temporal structure.
This limitation is acknowledged in Section~\ref{sec:discussion} and
constitutes a direction for future work.
```

### 3.3 修正路径（推荐，中期实施）

采用 Aloni et al. ([2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envsoft.2024.106283)) 的 DFT 相位随机化方法修正合成数据生成器：

**具体步骤：**

1. 以 AntAWS 数据集中某代表性站点（如 D17）的多年风速记录为基础信号，计算其 DFT 幅度谱 $|X(f)|$
2. 生成随机相位 $\phi(f) \sim \mathrm{Uniform}(-\pi, \pi)$，构造合成信号 $\tilde{X}(f) = |X(f)| e^{i\phi(f)}$
3. 对 $\tilde{X}(f)$ 做逆 DFT，得到保留原始 ACF 结构的合成风速序列
4. 通过参数 $m$（保留的低频相位数量）控制合成序列与原始序列的相似度

该方法可严格保证合成序列的 ACF 与原始信号一致，从而满足命题 3 的全部标准。修正后需重新生成训练数据并重跑 S1 实验，建议作为 V3 版本的主要改进。

**§4 命题 3 修改建议（修正后版本）：**

```latex
\begin{proposition}[Synthetic process fidelity]
  The DFT-phase-randomised synthetic time series $\{\tilde{x}_t\}$
  satisfies: (i) identical marginal distributions to the reference
  AntAWS record; (ii) identical power spectral density, and hence
  identical autocorrelation function; (iii) blowing-snow event
  frequency consistent with \citet{Amory2020} via CRED-conditioned
  Bernoulli triggering.
\end{proposition}
```

## 四、审稿意见四：预言机在部分观测输入下的预报精度评估

### 4.1 问题定位

审稿人要求验证：当 oracle（TCN 预报模型）仅接收部分观测输入（即调度器选择的传感器子集）时，其预报精度是否仍然可靠，以确认奖励信号 $r_t = -\mathrm{MAE}(\hat{y}_{t+h}, y_{t+h})$ 的有效性。

V2 实验报告中 S1 主结果表已间接回答了这一问题：Full observation（FW-MAE=0.3689）与 PD-PPO（FW-MAE=0.3911）之间的差距约为 0.022，说明在 PD-PPO 选择的传感器子集下，oracle 的预报精度仅下降约 6%，奖励信号仍然有意义。

### 4.2 建议增加的实验（E2：Oracle 鲁棒性评估）

在 §6 中增加一个小节，报告 oracle 在不同观测完整度下的预报精度：

```latex
\subsection{Oracle Reward Signal Reliability}

To validate that the prediction-driven reward remains informative under
partial observation, we evaluate the TCN oracle's forecast accuracy as
a function of the number of active sensor channels. Table~\ref{tab:oracle_robustness}
reports FW-MAE for the oracle conditioned on $k \in \{1, 2, 3, 4, 5\}$
active channels (averaged over all $\binom{5}{k}$ subsets), compared
with the full-observation baseline.

The oracle's FW-MAE degrades gracefully from $0.369$ (full observation)
to $0.XXX$ (single channel), confirming that the reward signal
$r_t = -\mathrm{FW\text{-}MAE}(\hat{y}_{t+h}, y_{t+h})$ provides a
meaningful gradient for policy optimisation even under tight budget
constraints. The reward is not degenerate (i.e., does not collapse to
a constant) for any feasible budget in $[1.65, 1.75]$.
```

**注意**：上述 `0.XXX` 需从实验数据填入。若当前实验未运行此评估，可从 S1 的 full observation 行（FW-MAE=0.3689）和 random 行（FW-MAE=0.4321）之间的单调性推断：random 调度等价于随机选择传感器子集，其 FW-MAE 上界即为 oracle 在最差观测子集下的精度。

### 4.3 §5 方法论补充

在 §5 的 Prediction-Driven Reward 子节中增加以下说明：

```latex
The oracle $f_\theta$ is trained on full-observation sequences and
evaluated at deployment on the partial-observation inputs selected by
the scheduler. This creates a train-test distribution shift; however,
the oracle's architecture (TCN with masked input channels) is designed
to handle missing observations via zero-imputation, and the reward
signal remains monotonically informative as long as the oracle's
partial-observation accuracy exceeds that of a trivial predictor.
The full-observation FW-MAE of $0.369$ versus the random-scheduling
FW-MAE of $0.432$ (a $17\%$ gap) confirms that the oracle provides
a non-degenerate reward landscape for policy gradient optimisation.
```

### 5.3 §7 讨论节修改建议

在 §7 中增加一个专门面向极地科学读者的子节：

```latex
\subsection{Implications for Antarctic AWS Operations}

The 7.9\% improvement in forecast-weighted MAE over Age-of-Information
scheduling translates to a reduction of approximately $0.055\,^\circ\mathrm{C}$
in mean absolute temperature prediction error at the one-step-ahead
horizon. While this may appear modest in absolute terms, it is
operationally significant in the Antarctic context for two reasons.

First, blowing-snow onset is governed by a sharp wind-speed threshold
near $8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$ (CRED $> 0.5$; \citealt{Amory2020}),
and accurate temperature forecasting near this threshold determines
whether precipitation falls as snow or mixed-phase precipitation---a
distinction critical for surface mass balance estimation
\citep{Ding2025}. Second, the PD-PPO scheduler's ability to
preferentially activate the blowing-snow flux sensor ($\mathrm{FC4}$)
and the OTT~Parsivel2 disdrometer during high-wind-speed periods
(as evidenced by the EventAwareCritic's conditioning on $z_t$)
directly supports the real-time detection of saltation onset, which
is the primary trigger for automated data quality flagging in the
AntAWS dataset \citep{AntAWS2023}.

The present simulation is calibrated to the statistical properties of
the AntAWS dataset but does not yet incorporate the full physical
coupling between wind speed, temperature, and blowing-snow flux
captured by high-fidelity models such as CRYOWRF \citep{Sharma2023}.
Future work should validate the scheduling policy on CRYOWRF-generated
synthetic sequences, which reproduce the observed power-law relationship
between wind speed and blowing-snow mass flux \citep{Amory2020,Ding2025}.
```

The simulation environment is designed to reproduce the statistical

problem, rather than to serve as a high-fidelity physical model.
The marginal distributions of wind speed, air temperature, relative

the AntAWS dataset \citep{AntAWS2023}, which aggregates observations
from [N] Antarctic stations spanning [year range]. Blowing-snow event

$p_{\mathrm{event}} \approx 0.20$, consistent with the event frequency

$c_i$ (Table~\ref{tab:sensor_specs}), and the total budget constraint
