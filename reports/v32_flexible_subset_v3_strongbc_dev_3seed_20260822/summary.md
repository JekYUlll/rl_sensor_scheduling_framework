# Flexible-subset v3 strong-BC three-seed replication

The frozen configuration beat selected static on both endpoints in two of three
development seeds and beat the best conventional dynamic reference on both
endpoints in two of three. Mean margins were positive against static, but the
mean macro margin against the best dynamic reference was -0.018517.

All three runs passed feasibility and channel-use gates. Every channel had
intermediate duty, with 4--10 executed masks and switches per step between
0.012665 and 0.021421.

The result is not stable enough for fresh confirmation. Further strengthening
the four subtype prototypes would oppose the unrestricted-subset objective.
The next matched test instead uses the existing frozen-forecast greedy teacher
for BC warm start, allowing labels across the complete feasible action surface,
then retains the same normalized forecast reward and masked PPO optimization.
